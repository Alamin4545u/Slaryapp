About Nexora Tools

Clear information about our website and services.

Nexora Tools is an independent collection of practical browser-based utilities built to make everyday digital tasks simpler. Our aim is to provide clear, accessible tools without requiring visitors to create an account or install software. The collection includes image utilities, PDF helpers, text tools, calculators and everyday converters.

We believe useful web tools should be straightforward. Each page explains what the tool does, gives direct instructions and provides a result that can be reviewed before use. We work to keep pages responsive and usable across phones, tablets and desktop browsers. The website is delivered as a static site, which keeps the infrastructure simple.

Privacy is an important design goal. Supported file tools process selected files locally in the browser rather than uploading them to Nexora servers. Some tools load third-party open-source libraries from content delivery networks. If advertising or analytics are enabled, those providers may collect technical information under their own policies. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

Nexora Tools is provided for general convenience. Calculators and converters are informational and may not replace professional advice, official measurements or institution-specific requirements. We encourage visitors to verify important results before relying on them.

We welcome feedback about accessibility, usability, errors and ideas for additional utilities. Visit the Contact page to reach us. We aim to make Nexora Tools more useful through careful improvements and clear communication.
