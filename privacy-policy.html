Privacy Policy

Clear information about our website and services.

This Privacy Policy explains how Nexora Tools handles information when you visit nexoraworldonline.online. The website is a static collection of browser-based tools. We do not require visitors to register for an account to use the core tools, and we do not operate a Nexora file-upload service for supported local-processing tools.

Files selected for supported image and PDF tools are processed in your browser. File contents are not intentionally transmitted to Nexora servers. Your browser may store temporary data in memory while a tool is running. Downloaded results are saved through your browser. Do not use a shared or compromised device for confidential material.

The website may use local storage to remember interface preferences, such as your selected dark or light theme. This preference is stored on your device and is not an account profile. Your browser settings can clear local storage. The site itself does not need advertising cookies to operate its basic tools.

This website currently loads third-party advertising scripts and displays sponsored links. Advertising vendors may use cookies, device identifiers or similar technologies to deliver, limit or measure advertisements, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable consent requirements. Vendors may process information such as IP address, browser type, device signals and ad interactions. We do not control their independent data practices.

Analytics may be added to understand aggregate site usage, performance and navigation. If enabled, an analytics provider may receive technical information such as page views, approximate location derived from network data, browser details and referral information. Analytics should be configured with data minimization and any consent required by applicable law.

Some tools load third-party JavaScript libraries from content delivery networks. When your browser requests these resources, the CDN may receive normal connection information, including IP address and browser headers. The tools do not send selected file contents to these CDNs as part of the intended processing workflow.

You can control cookies and local storage through browser settings. Blocking third-party advertising resources may prevent some ads from appearing, and blocking tool-library resources may affect tools that depend on them. Sponsored links may take you to websites operated by third parties. Review those providers’ privacy notices and terms before interacting with their content. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children.

This policy may be updated when the website or its providers change. The effective version will be published on this page. For privacy questions, contact contact@nexoraworldonline.online.
