Understanding BMI Calculator

Body mass index, or BMI, is a screening measure calculated from weight and height. It is often used as one broad indicator in adult population health, but it does not directly measure body fat or diagnose illness. Nexora calculates BMI from metric or imperial measurements and displays general adult category labels. The calculation runs in your browser.

How to use this tool step by step

Choose metric or imperial units, enter weight and height and select Calculate BMI. The calculator converts measurements to kilograms and metres before applying weight divided by height squared. Read the number and general category in the result area. Check your selected units carefully because a mismatch can change the result substantially.

Benefits, practical tips and limitations

BMI has limitations. It does not distinguish muscle from fat or describe fat distribution. Adult reference categories should not be applied to children and teenagers, who need age- and sex-specific interpretation. Pregnancy and some medical circumstances also require different assessment. Treat the result as general information and discuss health concerns with a qualified professional.
